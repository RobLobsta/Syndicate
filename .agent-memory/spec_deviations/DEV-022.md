# DEV-022: unresolved art ships under a recorded development exception, not under D08-R1b

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Category:** spec_deviations
**Related Docs:** docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.1

**Status:** active

Supersedes: DEV-021

## Summary
DEV-021 recorded five structure models processed against D08-R1b with no recorded terms, and gave
the disposition `IMPL_SHOULD_CHANGE` — establish the terms. The project owner has instead directed
that an exception be made for early development. D08 is amended rather than quietly broken: **R1d**
now permits it, bounded and checkable.

## Details

**Deviates from:** the licence half of D08's source-art rule (D08-R1), recorded there as R1b, and now conforming to the exception recorded as R1d. R1b's "a model with no recorded terms is not processed" stands for
anything heading for distribution; R1d carves out pre-release development, and the deviation this
entry supersedes becomes conformance with the amended document.

**What the exception requires**, all three enforced rather than intended:

1. `LICENCE.md` declares `status: development-exception` and records what **is** known.
2. The status travels into `structure.json`, because the asset gate validates `assets/` and
   `art-source/` is not shipped beside it.
3. The gate emits **A512** per model — advisory by default, an **error** under
   `SYNDICATE_REQUIRE_LICENCE=1`, which any distribution pipeline sets.

**The user offered two routes and only one was taken.** "Either make one up or make an exception."
Fabricating an author or a licence is not available: an invented record outlives whoever wrote it
and converts a known-unknown into an unknown-unknown, which is strictly worse than the gap. Every
`LICENCE.md` still says the terms are unresolved and says what has to happen before anything ships.

## Rationale / Context
The alternative was to keep a subsystem nobody can look at. This session found three defects in
those structures that only appeared once they were on screen (DISC-077), which is the argument for
the exception made concretely.

## Impact
**Blueprint Disposition: BOTH_VALID.** R1b is right for a shipping build and R1d is right for a
pre-alpha one; they are the same rule at two points in a project's life.

- `assets/structures/` still may not be distributed. `SYNDICATE_REQUIRE_LICENCE=1` fails the build
  if anyone tries.
- Five A512 advisories are expected in every development gate run.
