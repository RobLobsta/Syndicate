# DEV-009: a hanging part gets no constraint unless it is already a separate body

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Category:** spec_deviations
**Related Docs:** docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.6, docs/07_damage_destruction_model.md#D07-S5.7

**Status:** resolved

## Summary
**Resolved 2026-08-17.** D06-R21's second row and new R21a now say what `DetachSystem` does.

D06-R21 gives a destroyed-but-not-yet-detached part a `btFixedConstraint` so it hangs by a thread and
breaks under load. A constraint joins two rigid bodies, and an attached part is not one — it is
geometry inside the vehicle's compound (D06-R20, DEC-004). `DetachSystem` therefore creates the
constraint only for a part that already has its own body; for every other part the `HANGING_TICKS`
delay alone is the hang.

## Details

**Deviates from:** `docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.6` (D06-R21), the constraint table's second
row — *"Partially detached part (`DESTROYED` but not yet detached): `btFixedConstraint`
(short-lived, ≤ `HANGING_TICKS` = 60) with break impulse"* — and D07-S5.7's unconditional
`ensureHangingConstraint` in T1.

D06 contradicts itself, and D06-R20 is the load-bearing half: *"Attached parts are not separate
bodies (D05-R3) … in the normal case, no constraint exists for an attached part."*
`btFixedConstraint(rbA, rbB, frameInA, frameInB)` needs two bodies, and compound geometry has none.
The three ways to supply one all cost more than the feature:

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Remove the part from the compound and give it a body while leaving it in the slot graph | Splits the invariant that graph and compound describe the same parts. `PartDetachment` warns on that drift, `MassPropertySystem` sums the graph, and hit resolution maps compound child indices to slot paths (D07-S5.1) — three things needing a new state, for a second of drama |
| Detach immediately and constrain the debris body to the chassis | The part's mass leaves a second before it visually falls, so handling changes while the part looks attached. D05-R23 and G10 are about exactly that correspondence |
| Drop the hanging branch | Loses authored behaviour content can already request: `hangsBeforeFalling` is in D08-S4.2's part schema |

**Implemented:** `hangsBeforeFalling` comes from the loaded `PartType`. Such a part stays in the
graph, in the compound, at full mass, until `tick − stateEnteredTick >= HANGING_TICKS` (60), then
detaches with reason `DESTROYED`. `ensureHangingConstraint` builds the constraint through
`PhysicsWorld.attachBreakable` — threshold in N·s (D06-R22), 20 solver iterations, linked-body
collisions off — but only when part and vehicle both have bodies, which today only the articulated
case of D06-R21's *first* row would produce. T2 (a broken constraint detaches its part) is complete
and is what the constraint is for in either row.

**Consequence:** a hanging part is held rigidly, not by a joint, so a hit cannot knock it off early —
it falls when its 60 ticks are up, and T2 never fires for it. Separately, a part that both hangs and
fractures never hangs: `FractureSystem` (13) has no hanging check, so it shards any `DESTROYED` part
with fracture data before slot 14 sees it. Hanging is a non-fracturing part's behaviour.

## Rationale / Context
A session implementing `SpawnSystem` or revisiting D06-S5.6 will look for the promised
`btFixedConstraint`, not find one on a hanging plate, and either "fix" it by splitting the graph from
the compound — quietly breaking mass properties and hit resolution — or conclude the path is
unimplemented and rewrite it.

## Impact
`game-core` `system` (`DetachSystem`), `physics` (`PhysicsWorld.attachBreakable`), `asset`
(`PartType.hangsBeforeFalling`). Affects D06-S5.6 (D06-R21) and D07-S5.7 T1.

**Blueprint Disposition: DOC_SHOULD_CHANGE.** D06-R21's second row assumes a partially detached part
is a separate body, which D06-R20 and D05-R3 deny. The row should say a hanging part is held by the
compound for up to `HANGING_TICKS`, with the `btFixedConstraint` applying to the articulated case
only. Amendment deferred to the session that writes `SpawnSystem` — the first able to construct an
articulated part and see which reading the game wants.
