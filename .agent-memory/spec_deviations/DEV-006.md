# DEV-006: test_complex_hollow has no internal cross rib and weighs 2127 kg, not 2500

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Category:** spec_deviations
**Related Docs:** docs/14_test_environment.md#D14-S7.1, docs/09_blender_destruction_tool.md#D09-S12

**Status:** active

## Summary
D14-R20 describes `test_complex_hollow` as a hollow 1 m box with 0.05 m walls **and one internal cross rib**, at ≈ 2500 kg. The checked-in fixture is the shell alone: no rib, 0.2710 m³, 2127.4 kg at steel density. The gap predates this session and was never recorded.

## Details

**Deviates from:** (D14-R20), the fixture table row for `test_complex_hollow`, on two linked values — the geometry description and the expected mass.

`fixtures.complex_hollow` builds an outer 1 m box plus an inward-wound 0.9 m inner box, which is 1 - 0.9³ = 0.271 m³. The rib D14-R20 names is simply absent. The mass follows: 0.271 × 7850 = 2127.4 kg against the ≈ 2500 kg the table states.

**What the rib would be.** A 0.05 m slab spanning the cavity splits it into two 0.425 × 0.9 × 0.9 boxes, giving 1 - 2(0.425 × 0.9 × 0.9) = 0.3115 m³ = 2445 kg — which is what "≈ 2500 kg" describes. The construction stays a clean closed surface with no coincident faces: outer box plus *two* inward-wound cavity boxes, exactly the pattern already used for one. `test_decompose.test_two_disjoint_cavities_decompose_exactly` builds that geometry and decomposes it exactly, so the tool side is not what is blocking it.

**Why it was not changed here.** Regenerating a checked-in fixture binary and moving its expected mass is a separate change from resolving DEV-004, and folding the two together would make neither reviewable. The fixture as it stands does exercise what its "Exercises" column is mostly about — `signedVolume` on a non-convex closed mesh, and mass that is not the bounding box's — so nothing is currently untested because of this.

**What is affected when it is fixed.** `fixtures/meshes/test_complex_hollow.glb` is regenerated, so its `sourceHash` and `topologyHash` change and any golden manifest for it (D14-R23; none is checked in yet, per PROG-002) must be regenerated with it under D14-R24.

## Rationale / Context
An unrecorded deviation is read by the next session as intent: someone comparing the fixture against D14-R20 either "corrects" the document to match the mesh, or rebuilds the mesh without realising the mass in the table is a consequence of the rib rather than an independent number. Recording both, with the arithmetic, makes either fix a decision rather than a guess.

## Impact
`blender-tool/syndicate_fracture/fixtures.py` (`complex_hollow`), `fixtures/meshes/test_complex_hollow.glb`, and the D14-S7.1 fixture table.

**Blueprint Disposition: IMPL_SHOULD_CHANGE.** The document is right and specific: the rib is there to force interior structure into the fracture, and the mass in the table is the rib's arithmetic. The generator should build it.
