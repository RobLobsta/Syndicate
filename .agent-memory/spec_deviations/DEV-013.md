# DEV-013: art-source holds whole-vehicle glTF in a bucket D08-R1 does not name

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Category:** spec_deviations
**Related Docs:** docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.1, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.6, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S4.1

**Status:** active

## Summary
`art-source/` gains a `vehicles/` directory holding two whole-car glTF models — body and wheels in
one mesh — rather than the per-part `.blend` files D08-S4.1's tree is made of. They are source art
that has not been split into parts yet, and there was nowhere in the specified layout to put them.

## Details

**Deviates from the source-art layout (D08-R1)** in two ways. Its tree lists `art-source/parts/`, `arenas/` and `shared/`,
with no bucket for a model that is a whole vehicle — in the finished pipeline there is no such thing,
because a vehicle is an assembly of parts (D05-S4.1), not a model. And it says `.blend` sources live
here; these are glTF 2.0 separate (`scene.gltf` + `scene.bin` + `textures/`), because that is how
they arrived. D08-R1's actual rule is unchanged: nothing here is loaded at runtime, and D02-E12's
check that `art-source/` is absent from a distribution build still applies.

**Why not `assets/parts/`.** Because they are not parts. Each is one mesh containing the body and all
four wheels, and the game needs five parts with their own masses, health, damage states and
detachment. A whole car dropped into `assets/parts/chassis_eclipse_01/mesh.glb` would load — the
reader takes the file as one hull — and produce a chassis weighing the whole car with wheels welded
on.

**Each directory also holds** a `SOURCE.md` with provenance, the attribution its author requires and
every measurement taken off the model, and an `import.json` correction (DEC-036).

**Licensing, which D08 does not cover at all.** Both models are CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0: attribution
required, non-commercial, share-alike. They are prototype art, and a part split out of one inherits
the licence. The credit lines are in each `SOURCE.md`; the constraint is stated in
`art-source/README.md`.

## Rationale / Context
Without this, the bucket reads as somebody who did not read D08-S4.1, and the next session either
"corrects" it by moving the files into `assets/parts/` — which produces a car-shaped chassis with no
wheels of its own — or deletes it. It also records that the non-commercial term exists, which is the
kind of thing that is very expensive to discover late.

## Impact
`art-source/vehicles/`, `art-source/README.md`, and the future Blender split step that turns these
into `assets/parts/*/mesh.glb`. Affects D08-S4.1.

**Blueprint Disposition: DOC_SHOULD_CHANGE.** D08-S4.1's tree assumes art arrives already
part-shaped, and the first real art did not. A source bucket for unsplit whole-vehicle models is a
normal stage of an art pipeline, and the specification should name it — along with a line on
third-party licence terms, which it currently has nowhere to record. Left as a deviation rather than
amended in this session because the split step does not exist yet, and what the bucket should
finally look like is better decided when something reads from it.
