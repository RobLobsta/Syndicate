# DEV-003: Hull simplification maximises remaining volume, not minimum volume increase

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Category:** spec_deviations
**Related Docs:** docs/09_blender_destruction_tool.md#D09-S5.5, docs/14_test_environment.md#D14-S6.4

**Status:** active

## Summary
D09-S5.5 says hull simplification repeatedly drops the vertex whose removal *increases* the hull volume least. That rule is correct for simplifying a concave outline and wrong for a convex hull, where removing any vertex can only shrink it — so as written it selects the single most destructive removal at every step. The implementation maximises the remaining volume instead, then re-inflates to restore enclosure.

## Details

**Deviates from:** D09-S5.5 `simplifyHull`, the greedy rule stated under (D09-R14).

**Observed:** the cylinder fixture's part hull ended 0.61 m inside its own source mesh, failing `validateHull`'s enclosure condition by 300x the `HULL_ENCLOSE_M` tolerance.

**What changed:**
1. `simplify_hull` picks the removal that leaves the largest volume — the same intent ("change the shape as little as possible") applied to the convex case.
2. `inflate_hull` scales the simplified hull about its centroid by `1 + margin / r_min`, where `r_min` is the closest any face plane comes to the centroid. That moves every plane outward by at least `margin`, so enclosure is guaranteed by construction rather than checked and hoped for. Simplifying a curved hull *always* leaves it inside its source, so some inflation is not optional.
3. Above an 8-vertex reduction the greedy loop is replaced by direction sampling (keep the extreme vertex along each of `max_verts` Fibonacci-sphere directions). The greedy loop re-hulls once per candidate per step — O(n^3) — and took minutes on a 362-vertex sphere hull.

## Rationale / Context
The failure is silent in every check except enclosure: a hull simplified the wrong way still has positive volume, a legal vertex count, and a plausible mass. Only the enclosure test catches it, and only on a curved source — a cube-only fixture set would have shipped the bug.

## Impact
`blender-tool/syndicate_fracture/geometry.py`, `hulls.py`, and the D09-S5.5 pseudocode.

**Blueprint Disposition: DOC_SHOULD_CHANGE.** D09-S5.5's wording describes polygon simplification; the convex case needs the opposite comparison plus an inflation step the section does not mention.
