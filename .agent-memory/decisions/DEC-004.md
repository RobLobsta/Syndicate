# DEC-004: A vehicle is one rigid body with a compound shape, not one body per part

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S4.1, docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.3
**Status:** active

## Summary
Each vehicle is a single `btRigidBody` whose collision shape is a `btCompoundShape` of per-part convex hulls. Parts become independent bodies only at the moment they detach.

## Details
The obvious reading of "parts are individually damageable" is one body per part joined by constraints. That is both slower (30 constrained bodies per vehicle × 12 vehicles) and less stable in Bullet than one compound body. The gameplay requirement is satisfied instead by removing children from the compound on detachment (D05-S5.5) and recomputing mass properties in the same tick (G10).

A direct consequence: hit resolution uses the contact's compound child index to identify the struck part (D07-S5.1), and compound child indices are positional — `removeChildShapeByIndex` swaps the last child into the removed slot, so the slot-path map must be rebuilt after every removal (D06-R14).

## Rationale / Context
Someone reading "each part has its own mass and health" will reasonably assume each part is its own body and may refactor toward that. The performance and stability cost only shows up with many vehicles under destruction load, which a small test scene will not reveal.

## Impact
`physics` and `vehicle` packages, hit resolution, mass property recomputation, and the vehicle integration checks in D14-S5.7.
