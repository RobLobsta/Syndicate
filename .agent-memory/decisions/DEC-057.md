# DEC-057: a V's gain imbalance scales with bank unevenness and has no floor

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S8, D15-R38a8

**Status:** active

## Summary
The detune and the manifold delay between a V's banks keep their 10% floor, so no two banks are
ever identical. The **gain** imbalance loses it: it now scales purely with how unevenly the banks
fire, which is zero for every arrangement here except the cross-plane V8.

## Details
R38a8 requires every asymmetry between banks to scale with how differently they fire. All three
were scaled by the same `divergence`, which carries a `BANK_DIVERGENCE_MIN` of 0.10 so that even a
matched V has some. That floor was capping the one arrangement the rule exists to serve: the V6's
odd sub-orders crossed T-D15-13's −12 dB bound the moment the ceiling moved past 0.22, and the V8's
lope was held down with it.

The floor is right for the detune and the delay and wrong for the gain, and the reason is physical
rather than a tuning convenience. Detune and delay **decorrelate** two banks — they change how the
two patterns combine, so a little of each is honest on any V, whose pipes really do differ. A gain
difference does not decorrelate anything: two banks firing the *same* pattern at different levels
sum to that same pattern scaled, so the imbalance creates no sub-orders at all. Giving an
even-firing V a gain imbalance therefore invents a lope with no source, and the floor was doing
exactly that.

With the floor removed the ceiling could go 0.22 to 0.45 with all 17 tests still passing, and the
V8-over-V10 lope contrast improved from +2.8 dB to +4.9 dB — the change makes the cross-plane V8
*more* distinct, not less, which is the test of whether an asymmetry is in the right place.

## Rationale / Context
This is the fourth time bank asymmetry has been got wrong (DEC-054 records three). The pattern in
all four is the same: a constant applied where a scaling belongs, or a scaling applied to a
quantity whose physical effect does not depend on it. R38a8 now needs reading as two rules — scale
with unevenness, and only give a floor to asymmetries that decorrelate.

## Impact
- `EngineSynth`: `bankGainImbalance` uses `unevenness`, not `divergence`.
- `BANK_GAIN_IMBALANCE_MAX` 0.22 to 0.45.
- V8 rumble at the matched reference speeds 7.15/7.59 to 7.58/8.05.
