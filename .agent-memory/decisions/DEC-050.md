# DEC-050: slot 22 interpolates the ticks this process ran, not snapshots from a wire

**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/03_runtime_modes.md#D03-S5.3, docs/10_networking_multiplayer.md#D10-S5.6, docs/04_entity_component_model.md#D04-S4.3.5

**Status:** active

## Summary
`InterpolationSystem` (slot 22) samples every entity's world transform once per tick and rebuilds a render transform `alpha` of the way between the last two samples. It does not read `InterpolationComponent`'s snapshot ring buffer, which stays empty until slot 19 exists to fill it.

## Details
D04-S4.3.5 gives `InterpolationComponent` a `RingBuffer<TransformSample>` and cites D10-S5.6, which is *snapshot* interpolation: remote entities arriving at the snapshot rate, played back behind a delay. That needs a transport, and there is none.

What a single-player or listen-server client needs is different and is what D03-S5.3's `alpha` is defined for: the simulation ran a whole number of `TICK_DT` steps this frame and the frame sits somewhere between the last one and the next. Slot 22 therefore keeps a client-side `RenderTransformComponent` holding the previous and current world transforms, decomposed into position/rotation/scale at sample time, and lerps/slerps between them per frame.

Three details worth keeping:

- **Sampling is guarded on the tick number.** This is a per-frame system; sampling unconditionally would make "previous" one *frame* old rather than one tick old, and the interpolation would do nothing. `InterpolationSystemTest.severalFramesInOneTickShareOneSamplePair` is that guard's test.
- **Every entity with a transform is interpolated, parts included.** Slot 21 recomposes a part's world matrix from its parent every tick, so interpolating parts independently lands them where interpolating the parent and recomposing would — without slot 22 needing to know the slot graph.
- **A step longer than `SNAP_DISTANCE_M` snaps.** A respawn is a discontinuity, and smearing a car across the arena over one frame reads as a rendering fault.

The two mechanisms are complementary rather than alternatives. When slot 19 lands, it fills the ring buffer for remote entities and slot 22 gains a second source; the local path stays as it is, because a listen server's own vehicles are never in a snapshot.

## Rationale / Context
A future session reading D04-S4.3.5 will find a component built for interpolation sitting unused beside a system called `InterpolationSystem`, and is likely to "fix" it by routing local transforms through the ring buffer. That would put local motion behind a snapshot delay for no benefit.

## Impact
- `game-client` `present`, `component`: `InterpolationSystem`, `RenderTransformComponent`.
- `game-core` `InterpolationComponent` is untouched and remains unused.
- Slot 26 draws from `RenderTransformComponent.renderMatrix`, never from `TransformComponent.worldMatrix`.
