# DEC-078: a lamp is content, its light is cosmetic, and a beam is two things

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S5.11, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.2, docs/03_runtime_modes.md#D03-S5.1

**Status:** active

## Summary
A `light` part is authored with a `light` block giving its colour, cone, range, direction, origin
and whether it casts. The client reads that block itself, places a spot light per lit lamp, and
draws a translucent cone from the lens. `game-core` never learns any of it.

## Details
**The data is content; the lighting is not.** Where a lamp points and how far it throws are
properties of the art, so they are authored by the preparation pipeline from the part's own
geometry (D15-R51) rather than inferred by the renderer from a part id. But nothing in the
simulation can observe a photon, so the block is read by `game-client`'s `PartLamps` — the same
rule the visual mesh and the morph target names already follow, and the reason `PartType` does not
grow a field.

**A lamp is extinguished by reading, never by writing.** It lights while its part is alive; a
destroyed or detached headlight goes dark because the damage model already did that work. G6 in
the direction it is usually got wrong.

**A visible beam and a lit surface are two different things.** A spot light makes a headlight light
something; it does not make the beam visible, because there is no participating medium — a real
beam is seen because the air scatters it. So a translucent additive cone is drawn from the lens as
well, at the **inner** cone angle and well short of the lamp's range, because illumination reaches
further than scattering does.

**Only head lamps cast.** A tail light glows and illuminates nothing, which is true of the real one
and is what keeps eight vehicles to sixteen casting lights rather than fifty. The nearest eight to
the camera are given shader slots; the rest are drawn as beams only.

## Rationale / Context
Headlights are the first thing in this project that needs the scene to be dark, so
`RenderEnvironment` gains a night fraction — sun, ambient, sky colour and beam opacity all scale
with one number. It is a launch option and a key today; time of day is properly an arena's
property and D16-S4 already reserves a `sky` block for it.

## Impact
- `PartLamps`, `VehicleLights`, `RenderEnvironment.setNightFraction`, `--night`, and `N` in a match.
- The PBR shader is configured for spot lights; libGDX defaults `numSpotLights` to zero, so without
  that a `SpotLightEx` is compiled out and a headlight silently lights nothing.
