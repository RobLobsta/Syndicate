# DEC-082: recoil and knockback are one momentum formula, and a rocket is its one exception

**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/17_weapon_system.md#D17-S5.12, docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.4, docs/01_product_game_design.md#D01-S4.4

**Status:** active

## Summary
Firing shoves the firer and being hit shoves the target, both from the same figure — the shot's
momentum, `projectileMassKg × speed` — and both applied **off the centre of mass**: recoil at the
muzzle, knockback at the contact point. `WeaponFamily` gains `projectileMassKg`, `nominalSpeedMps`
and `recoilFraction`, and `recoilFraction` is 0 for exactly one family.

## Details
Momentum rather than a constant is what makes one formula serve both a cannon and a machine gun: a
12 kg shell at 250 m/s carries 3,000 N·s and a 0.12 kg round at 600 m/s carries 72, so the cannon
produces a shove a player feels and the machine gun one that is correctly imperceptible. Nobody has
to tune "the cannon should kick" separately.

**`recoilFraction` exists for `ROCKET` and nothing else.** A rocket accelerates on its own motor
after leaving the tube, so the launcher never takes the round's momentum — its recoil is 0 while its
knockback is unreduced. Without this, the same formula has a rocket pod shoving a car as hard as a
cannon, which is wrong in a way a player notices immediately.

**Both go through `PhysicsWorld`'s pending-impulse queue** (DEC-012) and are drained by slot 10 in
entity-id order, never applied at the call site. An impulse applied mid-schedule makes the result
depend on where in the system order the call happened rather than on the tick.

**Off the centre of mass is the whole gameplay content.** A shell into a front wing spins the target;
one into the centreline shoves it straight. Applied at the COM, knockback is a number nobody can read.

## Rationale / Context
The user chose "both: recoil on firer, knockback on target" over either alone. Recoil is what makes a
heavy gun feel heavy to fire and knockback is what makes it feel heavy to be hit by; each without the
other is half the sensation.

`nominalSpeedMps` exists because a hitscan family has no travelling projectile to read a speed from
and a shotgun still kicks.

## Impact
- `WeaponSystem` (8) takes a `PhysicsWorld`; `ProjectileImpact.resolveHitscan` now returns its sweep
  so the caller can place knockback at the point that was actually struck rather than casting twice.
- Measured on the real world: a cannon shot changes an Eclipse's velocity by >0.5 m/s and outkicks an
  autocannon by more than 10×; an off-centreline hit imparts >5× the spin of a centreline one.
- `PhysicsWorld.queueImpulseAt` and `PendingImpulse` documented their offset as *body-space*; it is
  world-space, and the doc is now correct. Nothing was reading it wrongly yet.
