# DEC-068: doors are found by a geometric cue, not by per-model overrides

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-R6a, docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S4.3

**Status:** active

## Summary
The Stampede's doors were labelled `chassis`. They are added to C1 as a **door-shaped**
geometric vote rather than named in the Stampede's `parts.json`, because a pipeline whose
promise is "drop in a model" cannot require a human to measure boxes for each new model.

## Details
Two independent things hid them, which is why neither cue caught it alone:

- C1's panel rule needs a flatness above 0.86. A door *skin* is flat; a door as a downloaded
  model has it is the whole assembly — skin, frame, window surround and inner card welded into
  one shell — and the Stampede's measure 0.809.
- C3 read the material name `paint` and voted `chassis` at 0.595. Nothing voted for a door.

`parts.json` (D15-R8) exists for exactly this and would have taken ten minutes. It was not
used, because the failure is not specific to this model: every car has doors, they are always
painted body colour, and they are always thick enough to miss the flatness bar. Fixing it per
model means fixing it again for every model.

The cue is six conditions, each holding out a different near-miss: thin in x and in x alone
(≤ 0.22 of the body's width), not round, at least 0.18 of the body long, outboard of 0.72 of
the half-width, between 0.20 and 0.78 along the length, and centred between 0.25 and 0.85 of
the body's height. It votes `panel` at 0.75 — above C3's paint vote, below any override.

The numbers are fractions of the vehicle's own dimensions, which is what D15-R7 requires of a
threshold, and they are taken from measurements of both shipped cars rather than fitted to one.
The tests assert the negatives as well as the positives: an interior door card (same shape,
0.67 outboard), a wheel and a sill each fail a *different* condition, so the rule is not
surviving on the margin of a single number.

## Rationale / Context
The alternative — leaving doors to `parts.json` — was the cheaper change and is the one to
revisit if this cue ever misfires. It should not be revisited by widening the bars: a
threshold that has to move for a new model is a bug in the threshold (D15-R7), and the right
response to a model this misreads is a `parts.json` entry *for that model*, leaving the cue
alone.

## Impact
- Both cars now yield two hinged, dentable doors; the Stampede previously yielded none.
- `art-source/vehicles/stampede/parts.json` is unchanged, which is the point.
- D15-R6a records the measurements so the constants are not mistaken for taste.
