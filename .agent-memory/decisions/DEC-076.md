# DEC-076: the house style is a table, applied to materials before anything is measured

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S4.5, docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S5.9, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.3

**Status:** active

## Summary
`assets/materials/style.json` fixes, per surface role, the colour band and reflectance every vehicle
in the game is moved into. `syndicate_prepare` applies it as stage 1b — after the model loads,
before the geometry is corrected — so a photoscanned supercar and a flat-shaded cartoon come out
looking like one artist's work.

## Details
Nine surface roles: body paint, trim, chrome, glazing, rubber, lamps, grilles, interior, underbody.
Each carries a hue shift, a saturation ceiling, a value band, metallic and roughness targets, and a
grime amount. Colour is **clamped, not assigned** — a red car stays red, because the hue is gameplay
and the saturation is what makes it a toy.

Three constraints shape the implementation:

**It must not touch labelling evidence.** A material's name, alpha mode, base alpha, transmission,
backface culling and emission *strength* are all cues the ensemble reads (D15-S4.2 C2/C3) and they
outrank every geometric measurement. Normalising them would make every windscreen opaque. The pass
writes base colour, metallic, roughness and emission colour, and nothing else.

**How hard it pushes is measured.** Mean saturation and textured fraction, both weighted by
triangles. Saturated *and* textureless is a cartoon: its base-colour textures are disconnected and
the house colour replaces them, which is what reskinning means. Anything else keeps its textures and
is pulled part of the way. Weighting by triangles is what stops one bright badge from costing a
photoreal car its textures.

**It runs before everything.** Not because it needs to — it is indifferent to scale and axes — but
because every later stage destroys what it needs: separation shatters a material group into
thousands of shells, grouping joins shells from different materials, and the export writes one mesh
per part. By then there is no material left to normalise.

## Rationale / Context
The concrete failure this prevents is a roster that reads as several games at once, which is the
default outcome of building a vehicle game out of downloaded art. It is not a rendering problem and
cannot be fixed at render time: the colours are in the files.

## Impact
- Both shipped cars measure as `realistic` (82% and 88% textured) and are pulled 55% toward the
  palette; a cartoon source would be fully reskinned.
- The report and each vehicle's `manifest.json` record which surface every material was classified
  as and why.
