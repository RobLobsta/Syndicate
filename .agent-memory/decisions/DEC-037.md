# DEC-037: the Stampede comes from a Mustang GTD, so the pair's contrast is mass

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.6, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.4, docs/01_product_game_design.md#D01-S5.7

**Status:** active

## Summary
`STAMPEDE`'s reference car changes from the 1289 kg Ford Mustang GT3 to the 1969 kg Ford Mustang GTD,
because the GTD is the car the supplied art actually is. The shipped pair is therefore a light car
against a heavy one rather than a road car against a race car, and the calibration tests assert the
new contrast rather than the old.

## Details

**What changed.** Every figure downstream of the reference: 1289 → 1969 kg, 410 → 608 kW, 570 → 900
N·m, 3.30 → 3.40 s to 100 km/h, 267 → 325 km/h, slicks → 325/30 and 345/30 ZR20 road tyres, and the
class from `medium` to `heavy`. `Cd·A` is now solved backwards out of the published 202 mph rather
than estimated from a class, which puts the derived top speed at 324.9 km/h against a published 325 —
the first time a shipped profile's aerodynamics have been a measurement rather than a guess.

**What it does to the design.** The old pairing was a road car that won the drag race against a race
car that won everything else. The new one is:

| | Eclipse (MC20) | Stampede (GTD) |
|---|---|---|
| Kerb mass | 1500 kg | 1969 kg |
| Power at road | 417 kW | 547 kW |
| Standing start | **wins** — 9.87 m/s² | 8.40 m/s² |
| Downforce | 0.20 N/(m/s)² | **1.30** |
| Grip | 2.00 | **2.50** |
| Brake force per kg | 11.7 N/kg | **12.9** |

Still a choice rather than a ladder, and arguably a sharper one: the trade is now legible as mass
against everything else.

**Two assertions changed with it.** `VehicleProfileCalibrationTest` asserted that the race car was
lighter and that it stopped shorter in simulation. The first is simply a different fact now. The
second does not survive the mass change either — see DISC-014 — and was replaced by the tolerance
checks it sat beside rather than by a larger friction number.

**What did not change.** DEC-033 still holds in full: the in-game name is the project's own, the
manufacturer's is a field, and no trademark appears in an asset id or a display name.

## Rationale / Context
A profile that names one car and carries another's numbers is worse than no profile, and the failure
is quiet — `VEHICLES.md` would have printed "Ford Mustang GTD" above a 1289 kg GT3's mass and nobody
reading it would have had a reason to doubt it. This records that the recalibration was deliberate
and complete rather than a rename that stopped at the string.

It also records why two tests lost assertions, so a future session does not read that as tests being
weakened to go green.

## Impact
`game-core` `vehicle` (`VehicleProfiles`), `assets/parts/chassis_stampede_01`,
`assets/parts/wheel_stampede_{front,rear}_01`, `assets/vehicles/vehicle_stampede_01`, `VEHICLES.md`,
`ShippedContent`'s stand-in hulls, and `VehicleProfileCalibrationTest`.
