# DEC-018: a detached part becomes debris from its own state, not from its trigger

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.5, docs/07_damage_destruction_model.md#D07-S5.7, docs/04_entity_component_model.md#D04-S4.3.1

**Status:** active

## Summary
`PartDetachment.detach` records each leaving part's world transform and the velocity it left with
(`v + ω × r`) on the part's own `Transform` and `Velocity`, and `DetachSystem` (14) turns every part
that is `DETACHED`, vehicle-less and bodyless into a debris body in one pass keyed on that state.
Neither half asks which trigger removed the part, or whether slot 14 removed it at all.

## Details
DEC-016 pushed D05-S5.5 step 4 — turning a detached subtree into world objects — out of `detachPart`
and onto the caller, because only the caller knows whether a part fractures or leaves whole. That
left the gap PROG-005 recorded: `FractureSystem` detaches a whole subtree and turns only its root
into shards, so the parts below it stayed bodyless and inert forever.

Having each caller spawn bodies for everything `detach` returns does not work. Once `detach` has
returned, a part's placement is unrecoverable: its transform was the slot chain it just left, and its
velocity was the vehicle's, measured from a centre of mass `MassPropertySystem` is about to move. So:

- **`detach` records, because only `detach` still can.** It derives the vehicle's
  chassis-local-to-world transform and world COM once, before the first node leaves the graph, and
  writes each part's world transform and `v + ω × r` onto the part — adding a `Transform` or
  `Velocity` where the part had none, which an attached part legitimately does not need. Not the
  spawn step DEC-016 moved out; the last moment the information exists.
- **Slot 14 embodies, driven by state.** Its third pass takes every part that is `DETACHED`, has
  `vehicleEntity == NULL` and no body, spawns one debris body from its cached hull at the recorded
  placement, and retires the part entity. A part that already had a body — D06-R21's articulated
  case — keeps it and gains a lifetime instead.

Reading state rather than a return value is what covers `FractureSystem`'s orphans in the tick they
are orphaned (13 runs before 14), and would cover any future detaching system too.

The debris body is a **new entity** and the part entity is destroyed, the same shape shards use.
Debris is cosmetic and unreplicated (D07-R5); one entity in both roles would leave a vehicle's former
parts addressable by systems with no business with scrap.

## Rationale / Context
Without this, the next session either re-derives the placement capture in every caller — two copies
of the COM recentring term, the piece of that math that is invisible when omitted, which is why
`PartPlacement` now holds it once — or reports the bodyless subtree as a fresh bug. It also explains
why `PartDetachment` writes two components D05-S5.5's pseudocode does not mention.

## Impact
`game-core` `vehicle` (`PartDetachment`, new `PartPlacement`), `system` (`DetachSystem`,
`FractureSystem`). Closes PROG-005's bodyless-subtree gap. Affects D05-S5.5 step 4, D07-S5.7.
