# DEC-045: the material says what a part is made of; the part says how it fails

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.3, docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S5.7, docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S8

**Status:** active

## Summary
`assets/materials/materials.json` is the single shared authority for density, per-damage-type resistance, fracture brittleness and — new — `audioMaterial`. `destructionClass` is **not** on it: it is a property of what a part *is*, so it lives on `part.json` and defaults from `PartCategory`.

## Details

The user's brief was "decide on the shared material". The decision has two halves.

**What belongs in the table.** One `materialId` answers every question about what a piece of a car is *made of*, and every tool reads the same file: the runtime damage formula, the Blender fracture tool (D09-S6.3), and the audio bank (D15-R37). The alternative — a table per concern, joined by convention — was rejected for the reason G7 exists. Mass conservation compares a part's mass against the sum of its shards', and that comparison is only meaningful because both numbers come from one density. A windscreen that clangs is the same class of bug and has no check at all when the two facts live in different files.

The shipped table is nine rows: `steel`, `steel_hardened`, `aluminium`, `composite`, `rubber`, `glass`, `plastic`, `trim`, `lead`.

**What does not.** The first implementation put `destructionClass` on the material and it was wrong. D15-R32 maps *label* to class, not material to class, and the counter-example is immediate: a chassis rail and a door skin can be the same steel and fail completely differently, because one is load-bearing geometry that buckles globally and the other is a panel that dents locally. `DestructionClass.forCategory` is the projection from the runtime's `PartCategory` onto D15-S4.1's label taxonomy, and a `part.json` may override it.

**A third axis, deliberately coarser than the first.** `AudioMaterial` has five values against the table's nine rows. Steel, hardened steel and lead are three things to a damage formula and one thing to an ear. Collapsing them is what keeps the sound bank at a size a person can author and mix (D15-R37), and it is why the audio family is a field rather than something derived from `materialId`.

## Rationale / Context
Without this entry, the next session extending the material table has to re-derive why `destructionClass` is on the part rather than beside `densityKgPerM3`, where it would obviously belong — and would probably move it, because the material row is where every other physical property lives. The concrete failure that follows is a chassis authored as `steel` receiving `SHEET_METAL`'s fine subdivision and squishing like a sponge, which is precisely the failure mode D15-S5.7's `structural` row exists to prevent.

## Impact
`MaterialDef`, `PartType`, `AssetLoader`, `assets/materials/materials.json`, and the audio bank's keying. D08-R7 gains R7a and R7b in the same commit.
