# DEC-011: A non-convex source is decomposed by solid BSP, exactly, not by V-HACD

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/09_blender_destruction_tool.md#D09-S5.2, docs/14_test_environment.md#D14-S7.1

**Status:** active

## Summary
A non-convex source is split into disjoint convex pieces by a solid binary space partition over its own face planes, so `cell ∩ source` becomes a union of exact polytope intersections. An approximate decomposition (V-HACD) and a per-cell mesh boolean were both rejected: each gives up the exactness that is the point of the convex path.

## Details

**The problem.** A Voronoi cell is an intersection of half-spaces, so `cell ∩ source` is one polytope intersection *if the source is convex* — the identity DEV-005's fix rests on. A non-convex source has no half-space representation, which is why it kept falling back to cutting the mesh and why DEV-004's hollow box measured 6.4x its own mass.

**Why the BSP terminates and stays exact.** Split planes come only from the source's own faces, and a chosen plane's coplanar faces are consumed by the split, so the number of distinct planes strictly decreases with depth. Every face normal points out of the material, so the region behind a face plane is material and the region in front is not; a subregion with no boundary face left in it is homogeneous, and which one follows from the side of its parent's plane. Each solid leaf's constraints, clipped against the source's AABB, *are* the piece. Measured: cube, plate, cylinder and sphere give one piece each (sphere 0.22 s), the hollow box six, exact to float precision.

**Alternatives, and why not:**

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| V-HACD or similar | Approximate: the pieces neither tile the source nor stay inside it, so mass conservation (G7) becomes a tuning parameter. It also needs a dependency inside a `bpy` module, where installing anything is not something the build can assume. |
| Blender's Exact boolean, per cell | Measured and rejected in DEV-005: at these operand ratios it silently returned the unmodified source for some cells, and results larger than either operand for others. |
| Tetrahedral decomposition | Not general (Schönhardt: Steiner points required), and far more pieces. |
| Signed tetrahedra from one apex | Exact for volume, useless for geometry: the negative terms have no mesh to render, hull or export. |

**Two consequences.** A shard may now be a *union* of polytopes, so its mesh can carry several closed components with coincident interior faces; volume, centroid and inertia are sums over closed components and are unaffected. And a shard's convex hull spans its concavity — 1.8x to 3.4x the shard's volume on the hollow box. Nothing gates that today; D09-S5.5's multi-piece collision is the eventual answer.

**Refusal is a first-class result.** The pieces' volumes must sum to the source's within 1e-4 relative, which catches a source that is not the closed, consistently wound solid a BSP needs — stage 1 only requires positive volume. That check and the piece and depth caps return a *reason* rather than raising, so an undecomposable source degrades to mesh cutting instead of failing the run.

## Rationale / Context
Without this entry the next session sees several hundred lines doing what V-HACD does in one call, and the obvious "simplification" silently trades exact mass conservation for a tuning parameter — surfacing much later as fixtures drifting against a 2% tolerance. The rejected list also records that the mesh-boolean route was already tried and measured.
