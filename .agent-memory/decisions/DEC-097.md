# DEC-097: a road's extent is checked against the rim height under its centreline

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/16_procedural_arena_generation.md#D16-S5.4, docs/16_procedural_arena_generation.md#D16-S5.5, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.7

**Status:** active

## Summary
`RoadCarver.validateExtent` rejects a road before the carve, by asking how high the border rise
stands under each station of its centreline and comparing that to `MAX_CUT_M`. It is the guard
DISC-062 asked a future session to write, and it is what let the scrapyard's withdrawn haul road
ship.

## Details
The rim height under the centreline **is** the cut depth, because a road that reaches the rim does
not climb it: the profile is smoothed over 25 m and then grade-limited, so it flattens to near the
arena floor and the blend drags the rim down to meet it. Checked against DISC-062's measurements on
the desert, whose rim is 40 m over a 60 m band:

| Spline extent | Rim under centreline | Measured cut |
|---|---|---|
| ±240 m | 0.0 m | 3.3 m (landform alone) |
| ±290 m | 34.3 m | 30.8 m |

So the same `MAX_CUT_M` serves both guards, one predicting and one confirming, rather than each
carrying its own number.

**Three things it deliberately is not.**

*Not a rule about how far a corridor may reach.* The first attempt rejected any corridor overlapping
the border band and immediately failed the **shipped** desert highway: its outer falloff does clip
the rim's outermost metres, where the rim has barely begun to rise, and that is both harmless and
visible in the arena as the road running into the hills.

*Not a replacement for `validateCuts`.* A measured cut is seed-dependent — the same over-long spline
digs 31 m on one landform and might dig 9 m on a flatter one, slipping under the limit while still
opening a corridor. The extent guard is seed-independent and fires first; the cut guard still catches
a canyon dug for any other reason.

*Not a remembered extent.* It reads `borderWidthM` and `borderRiseM` off the arena being generated.
`TerrainGenerator.borderRiseAt` was factored out of `applyBorderRise` so the rim's curve has exactly
one expression.

## Rationale / Context
DISC-062 cost a session because the damage surfaced three stages downstream, as D16-R58 rejecting
spawn connectivity or D16-R38 finding drivable ground at the arena edge, on eight consecutive seeds,
and neither message says the word "road". The guard runs once, before the first height is touched,
and names the road, the point, the rim height and the distance to keep.

It is also the concrete reason the scrapyard's haul road was withdrawn rather than authored: the two
arenas differ in size and rim, so a spline verified on one says nothing about the other, and there
was no way to check the second short of re-deriving the first by hand.

## Impact
`game-core` `arena` (`RoadCarver.validateExtent`, `RoadCarver.safeDistanceToEdgeM`,
`TerrainGenerator.borderRiseAt`), and `assets/arenas/arena_scrapyard_01/arena.json`, which now ships
a 235 m haul road cutting 3.6 m and filling 0.6 m at a 2.5% grade. Tested across twelve seeds,
because an arena with `seed: 0` draws a new landform every match (D16-R6b).
