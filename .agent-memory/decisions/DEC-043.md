# DEC-043: a part's mass acts at its collision mesh's centre, not at its slot

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.7, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.2, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.2

**Status:** active

## Summary
`PartType.centerOfMassLocal` is the AABB centre of the part's collision mesh, and both
`AssemblyLayout` and `MassPropertySystem` (15) weight a part's mass there rather than at its
attachment point. D06-S5.7's pseudocode already says `part.type.comLocal`; the implementation was
using the slot transform's translation, which is a different point on every part that is not centred
on its own origin.

## Details

**What was wrong.** A chassis's mesh origin is on the road at the centreline — the dissection puts it
there so slot positions and axle measurements share one frame (D08-S4.2). Taking that origin as where
the chassis's 1 100 kg acts put the whole vehicle's centre of mass at y = 0.059 m instead of ~0.67 m.
Bullet recentres the compound on the COM (D06-S5.7 step 2), so the car was rotating about a point
under the tarmac, and the suspension was pushing up 0.59 m above the mass it carried. The Stampede
lost 0.5 s off its published 0-100 to wheels that kept unloading.

**Why the AABB centre.** A convex hull's AABB centre is within a few centimetres of its volume
centroid for shapes as boxy as a car body, and D08-R5 authors no per-part centre. The alternative is
a `comLocal` field on `part.json`, which is a number no artist would get right and no test could
check independently of the art it describes. A wheel is unaffected either way: the dissection centres
one on its axle, so origin and centroid agree to a millimetre.

**What it changed.** Both shipped assemblies' `expected.comLocal` — Eclipse `(0, 0.672, 0.186)`,
Stampede `(0, 0.714, 0.004)`. D08-S4.4's own example assembly gives `y: 0.62`, so these are the
figures the blueprint expects a car to have; the old `(0, 0, 0)` was the shape of the bug.

**Where the authored form belongs.** When a part's real centre matters more than its bounding box —
an engine block, a fuel tank — D08-R5 is where to add it, and this becomes the fallback.

## Rationale / Context
Recorded because the code now computes something D08-R5 does not author, and the next session
reading `PartType` will want to know why a field that is not in the part schema exists. Also because
the failure it fixes was diagnosed twice from the wrong end: it looks exactly like under-damped
suspension, and DEC-034 says the damping figures are calibrated and must not be touched.

## Impact
`game-core` `asset` (`PartType`, `AssemblyLayout`), `vehicle` (`VehicleFactory.createPart`), and both
`assets/vehicles/*/assembly.json`. Affects D06-S5.7 step 1 and D08-S5.4's A311 check.
