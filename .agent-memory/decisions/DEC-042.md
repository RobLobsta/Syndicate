# DEC-042: a vehicle is dissected by geometry, never by the names in the file

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.1, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.2, docs/09_blender_destruction_tool.md#D09-S4.1

**Status:** active

## Summary
`syndicate_dissect` finds a vehicle's wheels by connected-component analysis and spatial
classification — outboard, low, and round in profile — and ignores every name in the source file. It
is a second Blender package beside `syndicate_fracture` rather than a mode of it.

## Details

**Why not names.** The two shipped models name their pieces `Object_170` and
`polySurface766_F:Ford_MustangGTD_2025_CallipersCalliperA_Zone_Material1_0`. The Eclipse does have
two nodes called `wheel_lf.child_100` and `wheel_lf.child.001_188` — and they are the front-left and
front-**right** wheels, because the author mirrored a subtree and Blender's `.001` suffix is the only
thing distinguishing them. A classifier that trusted that would put both front wheels on the same
side. The other two wheels have no wheel-ish name at all.

**What geometry gives instead.** A wheel never shares vertices with the sheet metal around it, so it
is a separate connected component; it is outboard of the centreline; it is low; and — the
discriminating test — seen from the side it is *round*, its vertical and longitudinal extents
agreeing to within a fraction. Sills, wishbones and exhausts are all low and outboard; none is round.

**Two thresholds are set by counterexample rather than by intuition**, and the module says so:
the height limit is 0.65 m because the Eclipse's wing mirror sits at 0.746 and passed at 0.80, and
the roundness tolerance is 0.25 because that same mirror is round to 0.34.

**Measured from the seeds, not from the group.** A wheel's axle and diameter come from the round
pieces alone; the caliper and hub that are captured afterwards travel with the wheel but do not
define it. Measuring from everything reported the Eclipse's front tyre as 1.31 m rather than 0.71 m,
and every downstream figure — the slot position, the wheel radius Bullet is given (DEC-022) — would
have inherited it.

**A separate package.** `syndicate_fracture` has a CLI contract fixed by D09 and one job. This runs a
stage earlier in D08-S5.1's pipeline, turns one input into five outputs, and shares nothing with it
but a Blender host. They are two packages under one project rather than a flag on one CLI.

## Rationale / Context
The obvious implementation is to look for a node called `wheel` and it will work on the next model
somebody downloads and fail silently on the one after — a mirrored subtree that classifies both left
wheels as right is invisible in a render and produces a car that drives sideways. This records that
naming was tried against the two models in hand, that it does not survive either of them, and which
specific pieces of geometry set the thresholds.

## Impact
`blender-tool/syndicate_dissect`, `assets/parts/*/mesh.glb` for both shipped vehicles, and
`:blender-tool:dissectVehicles`.
