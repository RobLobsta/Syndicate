# DEC-034: brake force is split across wheels by the load each is carrying

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.5, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.6

**Status:** active

## Summary
D06-S5.5 divides brake force equally across the wheels. `VehicleControlSystem` divides it in
proportion to each wheel's live suspension load instead, which is what an electronic brake-force
distribution does and what makes a shipped vehicle stop in roughly its published distance.

## Details
**What the equal split does.** Weight transfers forward under braking, the rear suspension unloads,
and Bullet clips each wheel's brake impulse to that wheel's own friction circle
(`m_wheelsSuspensionForce · dt · frictionSlip`). An unloaded rear wheel therefore throws away its
quarter of the brake, while the loaded front wheels — which could take more — are never asked for it.

Measured on the two shipped vehicles, 100-0 km/h against the published distance:

| | Equal split | By load | Published |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apex GT | 40.2 m | 36.6 m | 33 m |
| Stampede GT3 | 37.8 m | 29.9 m | 24 m |

The race car is the tell. On an equal split it barely out-braked the road car despite slicks and
downforce, which is backwards and would have been read as a tuning problem.

**Why load rather than an authored bias.** A front/rear bias is the usual answer and would need a new
authored field per vehicle. Splitting by live load needs none: the bias falls out of where the weight
actually is, tick by tick, and it handles damage for free — a vehicle down to three wheels brakes on
the three it has instead of sending a quarter of its braking to a corner that is gone.

**What is left.** Both vehicles still stop long — 11% for the road car, 25% for the race car. That
residue is the tyre model, not the split: no brake force recovers grip a ray-cast wheel does not
have. Closing it needs a tyre model rather than a bigger number, and the calibration test asserts the
ordering, which is what the design rests on, alongside a 30% band on the absolute figure.

## Rationale / Context
The diff replaces a division by four with a proportion and looks like a refinement. It is the
difference between a race car that out-brakes a road car and one that does not, and a session tidying
it back toward D06-S5.5's wording would undo that without any test failing loudly enough to explain
why.

## Impact
`game-core` `system` (`VehicleControlSystem`). Deviates from D06-S5.5's control-loop pseudocode in
the same spirit as DEC-028.
