# DEC-071: a structure is an assembly, so nothing new breaks it

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/16_procedural_arena_generation.md#D16-S7, docs/16_procedural_arena_generation.md#D16-S4.6, docs/07_damage_destruction_model.md#D07-S5.7

**Status:** active

## Summary
A destructible structure — a barrier, a sign gantry, a fuel bowser — is specified as an **assembly**
in the D05 sense: a root part with parts on its slots, ordinary `part.json` entries, ordinary fracture
manifests from the ordinary D09 tool. It differs from a vehicle in exactly one way: one **static body
per part**, rather than one dynamic body carrying a compound of every part (DEC-004).

## Details
The consequence worth recording is what it costs to break one. A structure needs **no new system, no
new component and no new schedule slot**:

- A hit becomes damage through `DamageApplication` (DEC-038), unchanged.
- A destroyed part fractures into its manifest's shards through `FractureSystem` (13), unchanged.
- Every part above it is now "a part whose parent is gone or destroyed", which is already a
  detachment trigger in D07-S5.7 — so `DetachSystem` (14) drops the span when the leg is shot away,
  and the sign with the span, for the reason it already drops a wheel.
- The debris expires through `LifetimeSystem` (16) and tears down through `EntityDestroySystem` (27).

The only addition is inside the detach path: a structure part's new dynamic body takes its mass from
its part definition, and its layer moves from `STATIC` to the debris layer in the same operation.

**Why one static body per part, against DEC-004's precedent.** A vehicle is one body because its parts
share an inertia tensor that must stay correct as parts leave (G10). A structure has none worth
maintaining and never moves as a whole, so the compound buys nothing — while the split lets a
destroyed part leave the world without rebuilding its neighbours' shape.

## Rationale / Context
The concrete failure this prevents is a `StructureSystem`. Faced with "make buildings destructible",
the natural move is a new subsystem with its own health, collapse rules and debris — and it would
work, and the game would then have two destruction models that drift apart, the second exempt from
every test and invariant the first is held to.

D16-R81 states the stop condition explicitly: if implementing structures requires a new system, a new
component, or a change to the damage pipeline, the design has drifted and the cheap version has been
lost. That sentence is the point of this entry.

## Impact
- Structures cost a factory and a placement pass, not a subsystem.
- Anything a vehicle part can do, a structure part gets: damage states, degradation, morph-target
  denting, propagation, material-driven density and audio.
- The load-bearing assumption is that a support chain is the slot graph. A structure whose stability
  is not a tree — an arch, a truss where two legs share a span — is not expressible, and the honest
  answer is to model it as a tree and accept that shooting one leg drops the span.
