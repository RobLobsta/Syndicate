# DEC-002: Chose the ray-cast vehicle model over rigid-body wheels

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.5, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.5
**Status:** active

## Summary
Vehicles use `btRaycastVehicle` — one rigid body plus N ray-cast wheels — rather than rigid-body wheels joined by `btHinge2Constraint`.

## Details
Rigid-body wheels are more physically faithful and would make a knocked-off wheel roll away for free. They were rejected because this game changes a vehicle's mass, centre of mass, and inertia constantly as parts detach, and constraint-based wheels need re-tuning against those properties. Under Bullet's sequential-impulse solver, heavy multi-constraint vehicles jitter and can destabilise — precisely during mid-destruction, the moment the game most needs to look right.

The visual benefit is recovered cheaply: a destroyed wheel is removed from the vehicle controller and re-spawned as a debris rigid body with its own hull (D06-R19).

## Rationale / Context
Without this record, the instability of constraint wheels would be rediscovered the hard way, most likely after someone tries the "more realistic" approach and spends days tuning it. The failure mode only appears under detachment load, so a naive prototype looks fine.

## Impact
`game-core`'s `physics` and `vehicle` packages, wheel index management (D05-R24), and the feel targets in D01-S5.5.
