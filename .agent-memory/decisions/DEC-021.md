# DEC-021: the spawn path establishes mass properties itself; slot 15 confirms them

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.2, docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.7, docs/04_entity_component_model.md#D04-S5.3

**Status:** active

## Summary
`VehicleFactory` computes total mass and centre of mass, recentres the compound on the COM, and
writes both to `VehicleChassisComponent` and `RigidBodyComponent` as part of building the chassis
body — rather than calling `MassPropertySystem.recompute` as D05-S5.2 step 4 does. Slot 15 then
compares equal on the spawn tick and does nothing.

## Details
D05-S5.2 ends with `VehicleStatsSystem.recompute(vehicle)` and `MassPropertySystem.recompute(
vehicle)`. D04-R13 prohibits system-to-system calls, and the same reasoning applies to a shared
operation reaching into a system: the schedule is meant to be the single description of causality.
DEC-017 resolved the identical conflict for `wreckVehicle` by letting the system that owns the work
run in its own slot.

Here it resolves more simply, because **there is nothing to defer**. `btRigidBody` cannot be
constructed without a mass and an inertia tensor, and a compound has to be recentred before
`calculateLocalInertia` is meaningful — so the factory has to do the D06-S5.7 arithmetic anyway. It
writes the results to the same components slot 15 writes, which is what makes slot 15 a no-op on the
spawn tick: `MassPropertySystem` compares its freshly summed mass and COM against the stored values
and returns early when they match (its own "no structural change" path). The first difference it
ever sees is the one a detach produces.

Two consequences worth knowing:

- **The spawn transform is shifted.** Recentring moves the body's local origin onto the COM, so the
  caller's world transform is post-multiplied by the COM offset to leave the mesh where the caller
  asked for it. A test asserting the body's world position gets the COM, not the mesh origin.
- **`VehicleStatsSystem` is not stood in for.** Its half of D05-S5.2 step 4 is genuinely deferred:
  `VehicleStatsComponent` spawns `dirty` and stays that way until slot 6 exists. Nothing reads those
  stats yet, so a spawned vehicle is a correct physical object that no input can drive.

## Rationale / Context
Without this, the next session reads D05-S5.2 step 4, finds no `recompute` call in the spawn path,
and adds one — which is both a D04-R13 violation and redundant work on every spawn. It also explains
why `SpawnSystemTest` asserts that slot 15 changes nothing on the spawn tick: that assertion is the
contract, not an incidental observation.

## Impact
`game-core` `vehicle` (`VehicleFactory`), `system` (`SpawnSystem`, `MassPropertySystem`). Affects
D05-S5.2 step 4. Extends the DEC-017 precedent from `wreckVehicle` to the spawn path.
