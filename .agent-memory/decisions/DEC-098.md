# DEC-098: a structure is cut by height, and the geometry is cut with it

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/16_procedural_arena_generation.md#D16-S7.1, docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S5.4, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.1

**Status:** active

## Summary
`syndicate_structure` cuts a whole-structure model into parts by **horizontal bands along Y**, then
by connected components within each band. A source object spanning more than one band is
**bisected** at the band planes and capped, rather than being sorted whole into the band its
centroid falls in.

## Details
Two passes, in that order. Bands first because a structure's support chain is vertical and the
chain is what the slot graph has to be; components second because two things standing side by side
at one height are two parts, either of which can be shot away while the other holds.

The bisection is the half that is easy to leave out and impossible to do without. Both city blocks
arrived as **one object seventeen metres tall**. Sorting objects into bands put the whole block in
the middle band and produced a single part, so "shoot the ground floor" would have deleted the
building — neither a collapse nor cover. The turret, which arrived as 138 separate objects at
different heights, needed no bisection at all and is why the failure was not obvious from the first
model tried.

Bands are sized by `BAND_TARGET_M = 6.0`, rounded, capped at six. Measured on the shipped content
that gives 5 bands for a 31 m turret, 3 for a 17 m block, 4 for a 22 m block and 1 for a 2 m tree.

## Rationale / Context
The alternative considered was D15's cue ensemble — eleven geometric cues that tell a wheel from a
door from a windscreen. A structure has no such variety: what makes it interesting is that it stands
up, and what stands up is a stack. Building a second cue ensemble would have cost a thousand lines
to answer a question with one axis in it.

The concrete failure this prevents is the one measured above: a building that is one part, which
looks completely correct in every document the tool writes and is wrong the first time anybody
shoots it.

## Impact
- `syndicate_structure` is a fifth the size of `syndicate_prepare`, because the cut is the only
  thing it decides; style, mass, hulls, morphs, shards and export are all reused.
- A structure whose support chain is genuinely not a stack — an arch, a truss where two legs share a
  span — is still not expressible, which is the limitation DEC-071 already recorded.
- Re-cutting is deterministic: bisected copies are named `<object>__b<n>` and every pass iterates
  sorted collections (G3).
