# DEC-100: a structure part is cut by how it fails, and masonry is its own class

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/15_vehicle_preparation_pipeline.md#D15-S5.7, docs/16_procedural_arena_generation.md#D16-S7.1, docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.3

**Status:** active

## Summary
Two decisions that only make sense together. A band's pieces are split into parts **by failure
family** as well as by footprint, and `MASONRY` is added to D15-S5.7 as a sixth destruction class.
Neither is useful alone: the split is what gives a building a glass part to shatter, and the class
is what stops the wall behind it denting like sheet steel.

## Details

**The split.** `bands.components` clusters a band's pieces by footprint overlap, which answers "what
stands together" — the right question for deciding what a part is. It cannot separate a curtain wall
from the concrete behind it, because they share a footprint exactly. `bands.split_families` runs
after it and answers a second question over the same pieces: "what fails together". Glazing comes
out as its own part; everything else is unchanged when a band has one material.

**The class.** D15-S5.7 had `GLASS` (shatters), `STRUCTURAL` (buckles), `SHEET_METAL` (dents),
`RIGID` and `NONE`. Masonry is none of them. It has no plastic range, so it cannot be `STRUCTURAL`;
it does not burst into slivers, so it should not share `GLASS`'s shard count. `MASONRY` takes
FRACTURE with 32 shards and no DEFORM, against glass's 24 and a tenth of the shell thickness.

**Consequences the split forced**, each a defect it exposed rather than caused:

- `graph.plan` had to learn that band 0's extra families hang off the root rather than becoming
  second roots (D16-R18 gives a structure one).
- `bearing_candidates` had to exist, because glazing was choosing glazing as its parent on
  footprint overlap alone — two independent support chains, and a free-standing column of windows
  left standing when the wall under it was shot out.
- HP and armour had to become material-aware (see Impact).

## Rationale / Context
The shipped city blocks had glazing in their source art the whole time — `Building_6_Glass` and
`glass_5` — and every part came out `concrete`, because `dominant_material` picks the material with
the most **surface** and on a building that is always the walls. So every pane in the game was
concrete that dented, and the one thing D15-S5.7 says glass must never do is dent.

## Impact
- The two city blocks go from 3 and 4 parts to 6 and 8: masonry and glazing per band.
- **Toughness became material-aware** (`mass.TOUGHNESS_BY_MATERIAL`). It had been flat, on the
  reasoning that material shows up in mass and in the damage resistances. That held while every
  part was concrete and broke the moment glazing was a part: mass and fragility move in *opposite*
  directions for glass, so a 2.3 t curtain wall was the toughest thing in the building.
- `brick` is a new material; the low block is authored in it and fractures, and the tall block
  stays concrete because its geometry will not fracture at all (DISC-076).
