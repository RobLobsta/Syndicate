# DEC-032: tractive force is capped by engine power, so a vehicle has a top speed

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S4.5, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.6, docs/06_physics_simulation.md#D06-S5.5

**Status:** active

## Summary
A fifteenth stat, `enginePowerW`, joins D05-S4.5's table. Available tractive force is
`min(engineForceN, enginePowerW / v)`: traction-limited at rest, power-limited once moving. Without
it, a chassis calibrated to a real car's 0-100 time reports a top speed several times what that car
has.

## Details
D05-S5.6 phase 4 derives top speed from `F_engine = k_drag·v² + k_roll·m·g`. That is correct only if
`F_engine` is the force the vehicle can sustain *at that speed*. The model applies one constant
force at every speed, so the two readings of `engineForceN` — the launch force and the sustained
force — collapse into one number, and they differ by nearly an order of magnitude.

Calibrating the first shipped vehicle showed it plainly. A 1500 kg car that reaches 100 km/h in
2.88 s needs about 14.8 kN. Feeding that into the top-speed formula with its real drag gives 637
km/h; the real car does 325. Calibrating the other way — force from top speed — gives 4.0 kN and an
eleven-second 0-100.

With the power term both come out right from published figures alone:

| | Force calibration | Power (90% of crank) | Derived top speed | Published |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apex GT | 14.8 kN from a 2.88 s 0-100 | 417 kW | 341 km/h | "over 325 km/h" |
| Stampede GT3 | 11.3 kN from a 3.3 s 0-100 | 377 kW | 267 km/h | ~267 km/h typical for the class |

The crossover — where `P/v` falls below the launch force — lands at 28 m/s for the road car and
34 m/s for the race car, which is roughly where a real car runs out of first-gear traction. That the
number falls out rather than being tuned is the sign the model is the right shape.

Two details. `P/v` is unbounded near zero, and an engine at rest is torque-limited anyway, so the
speed is floored at 1 m/s. A chassis declaring no power is unlimited, which is what content authored
before the stat existed still gets.

The reported `maxSpeedMps` is additionally clamped to `MAX_VEHICLE_SPEED_MPS` (D06-S5.5), because
every shipped vehicle out-runs the arena and a HUD should show the speed the game can deliver.

## Rationale / Context
A future session may see `enginePowerW` as redundant — the vehicle already has a force, and Bullet
never asked for a power. Removing it does not break a test that looks at acceleration; it breaks the
top speed, which nothing on screen shows yet. This records that the two numbers are not the same
quantity and that the second is what makes the first honest.

## Impact
`game-core` `vehicle` (`StatBlock`, `Degradation`) and `system` (`VehicleStatsSystem`,
`VehicleControlSystem`). Amends D05-S4.5's stat table and D05-S5.6 phase 4.
