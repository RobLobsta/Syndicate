# DEC-060: a part is exactly as relevant as the vehicle it is attached to

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/10_networking_multiplayer.md#D10-S5.10, D10-S4.3

**Status:** active

## Summary
`Relevance.isRelevantTo` answers for a part by asking the same question of its vehicle, rather than
measuring the part's own distance or treating it as an unknown archetype.

## Details
D10-S5.10's function enumerates the peer's own vehicle, the match, projectiles, vehicles and debris,
and returns false for anything else. Parts are none of those and are not mentioned — but they carry
the `healthFraction` and `DamageState` that R5 replicates, so "return false" would mean no client
ever sees damage.

Measuring a part's own distance would technically work and is wrong in a subtler way: a part and its
vehicle can land on opposite sides of the 400 m boundary for a tick, and the result is a visible car
whose damage stops updating, or a culled car still costing bandwidth for its parts.

## Rationale / Context
The general shape of the rule — replication follows the entity that owns the thing, not the thing —
will be needed again for the first weapon, whose ammunition is on a part.

## Impact
- `Relevance` recurses through `PartRefComponent.vehicleEntity`.
- A detached part (vehicle `NULL`) falls through to the archetype checks and, being neither a vehicle
  nor a projectile, is not replicated — which is right: by then it is debris (D07-S5.7, D10-R6).
