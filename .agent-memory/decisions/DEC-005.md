# DEC-005: Shard trajectories are cosmetic and are never replicated

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/07_damage_destruction_model.md#D07-S4.2, docs/10_networking_multiplayer.md#D10-S4.3
**Status:** active

## Summary
The authority replicates the *fact* of a fracture (part, tick, parent velocity) but never individual shard transforms or velocities. Each client spawns its own shard set locally.

## Details
Debris deals no damage (D06-R10) and blocks no projectile (D06-R11), so its exact motion cannot change any outcome. Replicating 12–64 shard bodies per destroyed part at 20 Hz across a 12-player match would dominate bandwidth for zero gameplay value.

The consequence to keep in mind: two clients may legitimately show shards in different places. They may never show a different damage state, a different part missing, or a different vehicle mass — those are all authoritative (D07-S4.2).

## Rationale / Context
A future session debugging "clients disagree about debris" could easily treat it as a desync bug and add replication for it, spending effort to fix something that is working as designed and making bandwidth worse. The inverse mistake is more dangerous: letting a cosmetic debris value feed back into gameplay, which breaks G6.

## Impact
`net` and `damage` packages, the replication tables in D10-S4.3, and the cosmetic-isolation CI check (D07-R6).
