# DEC-039: a weapon's identity is a block; its numbers are stats

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.2, docs/01_product_game_design.md#D01-S4.4, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S4.5

**Status:** active

## Summary
`WeaponBlock` carries what a weapon *is* — family, damage type, ammunition capacity, blast radius,
range, muzzle offset — and D05-S4.5's stat block keeps what a weapon *does*: fire interval, damage
per shot, spread, heat, projectile speed. D08-R5 was amended in the same commit, exactly as it was
for `HandlingBlock` (DEC-031).

## Details

**The dividing line is "can a utility part buff it".** D05-S5.6 phase 2 lets a utility multiply
another part's stats — an ammo feed shortens fire intervals, and `VehicleStatsSystem` already
implements that for `FIRE_INTERVAL_S`. Every number on that side of the line has to be a stat or the
buff cannot reach it. A family cannot: multiplying two weapons' families is meaningless, and summing
their damage types is worse.

**Five of D01-R8's seven fields were already stats** (`FIRE_INTERVAL_S`, `DAMAGE_PER_SHOT`,
`SPREAD_RAD`, `HEAT_PER_SHOT`, `PROJECTILE_SPEED_MPS`), which is what made the split obvious rather
than a judgement call. The two that were not — `damageType` and `ammoCapacity` — are in the block.

**Delivery comes from the family, not from a speed.** `WeaponFamily.Delivery` says whether a shot is
ballistic, hitscan, continuous or contact. Inferring it from `projectileSpeedMps == 0` would have
worked and would have turned a mistyped speed into a cannon that behaves like a laser.

**A stat resolves against zero, and the default is applied afterwards.** A stat's `add` term *is* the
authored value (D05-R15), so `resolve(FIRE_INTERVAL_S, DEFAULT)` adds a second to every weapon that
authored an interval. This cost one test failure to find and is the reason `statOrDefault` exists
beside `stat` in `WeaponSystem`.

## Rationale / Context
The next weapon anybody authors will want a number that is not on the list, and the question "stat or
block?" has one right answer that is easy to get wrong in the direction that quietly breaks utility
buffs. Writing the rule down makes it a lookup rather than a re-derivation.

## Impact
`game-core` `asset` (`WeaponBlock`, `PartType`), `vehicle` (`VehicleFactory`), `system`
(`WeaponSystem`), `shared-models` (`WeaponFamily`), and `docs/08_asset_pipeline.md#D08-S4.2`.
