# DEC-085: a lost feed leaves a burst chambered, and the fraction is 5%

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Category:** decisions
**Related Docs:** docs/17_weapon_system.md#D17-S5.13, docs/05_vehicle_part_system.md#D05-S5.4

**Status:** active

## Summary
D17-R61's feed row reads `ammoCapacity → 0` and "the weapon runs on what is chambered", which names a
quantity no document authors. A weapon whose feed is destroyed keeps **5% of its capacity, floored at
one round**, and gains nothing thereafter.

## Details
The row has to mean something between two readings that are both wrong:

| Reading | Why not |
|---|---|
| `ammoRemaining = 0` at the moment the feed dies | The weapon falls silent on one hit, which collapses the sub-part system back into a second health pool — the exact thing D17-R62 exists to prevent, one row above |
| Capacity goes to zero and the current count is untouched | With no reload mechanic anywhere in the game, nothing ever adds ammunition, so this is a no-op: a shipped autocannon carries 400 rounds and would never notice |

Five per cent, floored at one, is a belt-fed gun's receiver rather than its drum: the machine gun's
400 rounds become 20, and a cannon's dozen becomes one. It leaves the row meaning what it says — a
burst, then nothing — and it is never zero.

The number lives in `WeaponSubPartDegradation.FEED_LOST_CHAMBERED_FRACTION` beside the three
multipliers D17-R61 does author, so a tuning pass finds all four in one place.

## Rationale / Context
A future session reading `chamberedRounds` will want to know whether 5% came from a document. It did
not; D17-S5.13 is silent, and this entry is the answer to "why not zero", which is the reading the
row's own first clause invites.

## Impact
`game-core` `vehicle` (`WeaponSubPartDegradation`), `system` (`VehicleStatsSystem` slot 6). No content
change: no shipped weapon has a `feed` sub-part yet, so the row is reachable only by a weapon a future
model supplies.
